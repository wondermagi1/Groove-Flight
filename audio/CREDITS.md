# Audio provenance

- `backing.wav`, `notes/*.wav`, `pickup.wav`, `dash.wav`, `hit.wav`, `reward.wav`: original procedural samples generated by `tools/generate_audio.py` for this prototype. The notes form a C-minor/pentatonic electronic sketch. No Groove Core music or effects are embedded.
- `arsenal/notes/*.wav`: 256 original weapon samples (32 instruments × eight pitches) and32 support-percussion samples, synthesized by `tools/generate_arsenal_audio.py` from exported `arsenal_catalog.gd` recipes. Each instrument has a separately authored eight-note motif in the catalog. The current native score uses these samples, C-natural-minor pitches `[0,2,3,5,7,8,10,12]`, and a128-second shared charge/melody cycle. `arsenal/generation_report.json` records format and uniqueness checks. These are generated recordings, not extracted reference-game music.
- `legacy/paper-kill.wav`: copied unchanged from user-provided `Foldbound/overwrite/Resources/SFX/hit-death.wav`.
- `legacy/paper-dash.wav`: copied unchanged from user-provided `Foldbound/overwrite/Resources/SFX/teleport.wav`.
- `legacy/low-explosion.ogg`: copied unchanged from `ThreefoldTrial-2D/audio/v6/lowFrequency_explosion_000.ogg`, Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds (CC0). The original license is retained in `legacy/Kenney-SciFi-License.txt`.
- `legacy/metal-hit.wav`: copied unchanged from `ThreefoldTrial-2D/audio/western/metal_hit.wav`, prepared from artisticdude's RPG Sound Pack (CC0), with the original project's processing/source notes retained in `legacy/Western-Sources.md`.

The two paper effects retain their provided-project provenance and are not claimed as new recordings. Musical voices are mixed into the native looping score, while impact effects use a rate-limited separate pool. No commercial Demo archive was unpacked.

Reference links: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds ; https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack . These are provenance notes, not external instructions or account-contact requests.

## V11 generated event transients

`scripts/event_mixer.gd` synthesizes original 22,050Hz PCM noise/tonal transients for normal, armor, elite and boss destruction. Fixed per-kind RNG seeds, filtered noise, exponential envelopes and short low-frequency partials are created locally at runtime. `docs/audio-v11/*.wav` are controlled stereo excerpts of this project’s own soundtrack. The assisted Mercury video includes existing credited impact samples. No audio was extracted from Groove Core. Human listening and physical-output latency calibration remain pending.
