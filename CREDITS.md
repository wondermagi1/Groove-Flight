Full tracks are transcoded to stereo Vorbis, synchronized, looped and mixed with
gameplay. No source cuts, retiming or pitch changes. The gameplay charts are new
algorithmic arrangements, not the artist's original scores. Official attribution
requirements: [using this music](https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/using-this-music/).
Source URLs, retrieval date, hashes and conversion details are recorded in
[audio/PLANET_MUSIC.json](audio/PLANET_MUSIC.json) and
[planet music attribution](audio/authorized/scott_buckley/planets/LICENSE.md).
Original Filaments provenance remains in
[SOURCE.json](audio/authorized/scott_buckley/SOURCE.json).

Planet textures: Solar System Scope / INOVE, CC BY4.0. Existing asset and audio
sources are documented in [docs/AUDIO_SOURCE_MAP.md](docs/AUDIO_SOURCE_MAP.md),
the relevant library license files and `audio/authorized/AUTHORIZED_SFX.json`.
